Privacy Policy

After you agree, we record consent, app opens, gallery and settings visits, and errors to understand use and improve reliability. Events include random installation and event IDs, app ID, platform, version, time and days since first use with statistics enabled. We do not collect artwork, searches, feedback text or error details, or use these events for ad tracking.

Without consent you cannot enter the gallery. Turn off anonymous statistics in Settings to withdraw. This stops collection, clears pending events and the local installation ID, and returns you to this notice. It does not delete records already sent. Your artwork stays on this device.
